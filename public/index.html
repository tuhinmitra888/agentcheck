storeprobe

Can AI shopping agents buy from your store?

storeprobe tests whether AI shopping agents can find your products, understand them, and reach checkout, and shows you the evidence and the fixes that matter most.

In development · open source (Apache-2.0)

What it checks

  • Discovery: whether your products show up in the catalogs agents search, with the right price and details.
  • Product data: structured data, identifiers such as GTINs, and whether key facts are readable without JavaScript.
  • Protocols: whether agents can find your store's commerce endpoints (UCP, MCP, llms.txt).
  • Checkout: whether an agent can get the right item into a cart and reach checkout. storeprobe never completes a purchase.

Shopify stores first; other platforms later.

For store operators

If you see requests from storeprobe: it makes a small number of read-only requests, the way a crawler or a shopping agent would. It never pays, never completes a checkout, and only runs cart or checkout tests on stores whose owners have opted in.

Its UCP agent profile is at storeprobe.ai/ucp/agent-profile.json.
